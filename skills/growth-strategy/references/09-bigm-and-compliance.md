## 3. Moroccan & EU Marketing Compliance Checklist (Flag for Legal Review)

- **Loi 09-08 (CNDP — Morocco Personal Data Protection) & EU GDPR:**
  - B2B outreach must rely on public professional role contact data with documented legitimate interest and frictionless opt-out.
  - Any web/mobile flow capturing personal data, voice, photos, or behavioral analytics requires explicit consent notices and CNDP prior declaration/authorization verification.
- **Loi 31-08 (Consumer Protection):**
  - *Art. 21 (Misleading Advertising):* Prohibits ambiguous claims around pricing, conditions, or guaranteed outcomes—state thresholds and mechanics explicitly.
  - *Art. 23:* Sponsored content, influencer collaborations, and native ads must visibly disclose commercial intent (`"Publicité"` / `"Sponsorisé"`) and identify the advertiser.
  - *Art. 60–64 (Promotional Contests):* Promotional draws require written rules (*règlement*) and clear disclosure of prize quantities and values.
- **Loi 69-21 (B2B Payment Terms):**
  - Moroccan statutory inter-company payment terms default to 60 days (120 days max by written contract) with mandatory DGI declarations—verify any B2B deferred-payment offer against statutory rules.
