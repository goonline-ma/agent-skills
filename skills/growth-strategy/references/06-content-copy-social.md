# 06 — Authority Content, Conversion Copywriting & Social Atomization

Use when planning editorial calendars, drafting articles/landing copy/emails/scripts, building founder-led LinkedIn authority, or adapting campaigns across French, English, Arabic, and Darija.

## 1. Editorial Allocation: Searchable vs. Shareable

Every piece must earn distribution by being **Searchable** (captures active intent), **Shareable** (creates demand & executive trust), or **Both**:
- **60% BoFu / MoFu Searchable (Compounding Pipeline):** Problem-solution guides, migration runbooks, regulatory explainers, `X vs Y` comparisons, interactive calculators/templates. *(Apply the Information-Gain Gate from `03-seo-geo-search.md`.)*
- **30% Shareable Founder & Proof Narratives (Trust & Shortlist Velocity):** Shipped build teardowns, real field lessons, contrarian industry takes backed by math, benchmark snapshots.
- **10% Ecosystem / Partner Co-Marketing:** Joint playbooks with complementary partners (fiduciaires, industry associations, tech alliances).

## 2. Copywriting Frameworks Matched to Buyer Awareness

Never use the same formula for cold social readers and high-intent comparison shoppers:

| Buyer Stage | Best Framework | Execution Structure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unaware / Habit-Locked** | **Contrarian Truth / Cost of Habit** | Name the invisible tax of their accepted daily habit → quantify 12-month bleed → introduce the new category shift. |
| **Problem-Aware** | **PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solve)** | State exact symptom in buyer's words → trace downstream operational/compliance risk → reveal concrete mechanism that eliminates root cause. |
| **Solution-Aware** | **BAB + Unique Mechanism** | **Before** (manual chaos) → **After** (verified target state) → **Bridge** (the exact architectural reason your approach works where generic tools failed). |
| **Product / Most-Aware** | **Proof → Specificity → Risk Reversal** | Lead with peer outcome + concrete deployment timeline + security/compliance guarantees + friction-free start. |

## 3. Create Once, Distribute 6× (Atomization Pipeline)

Never draft one-off social filler. Anchor every week on **1 High-Substance Pillar Asset** (shipped case study, regulatory breakdown, teardown, or benchmark) and atomize into 6 native formats:
1. **Web / Blog Canonical Asset:** Search + GEO structured article with 40–60w answer blocks and JSON-LD.
2. **Founder LinkedIn Narrative Post:** Hook in lines 1–2 (before *"see more"* fold, ≤ 140 chars), concrete story/math in short scannable lines, 1 takeaway, zero external link in body (drop link in comment or edit if needed, or keep zero-click native).
3. **Visual Carousel / Architecture Card:** 6–8 slides or 1 dark-mode system/workflow diagram summarizing the framework.
4. **Short-Form Video Script (45–75s Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts):** Visual hook in 0–3s → 1 concrete problem → live screen/field proof → clear next step.
5. **Outbound / Newsletter Micro-Insight:** 120-word executive takeaway sent to CRM/prospect list.
6. **Sales Enablement Snippet:** Objection-handling answer block added to battlecards/FAQ.

## 4. Multilingual & Cultural Transcreation (FR / EN / Darija / AR)

Translate **intent and idiom**, never literal word-for-word syntax:
- **Moroccan Corporate B2B (CFOs, IT Directors, Fiduciaires):** Crisp executive **French** by default (precise regulatory terms: *ICE, PCGM, Loi 69-21, TVA à l'encaissement, CNDP*).
- **International / Tech / US Corridor:** Direct, active American **English**.
- **Moroccan Artisans, Retailers, Field Operators & Mass SMB Video:** Voice- and video-first (24.8% national illiteracy per HCP RGPH 2024 makes text-heavy flows underperform). Use **Darija**—Latin script for punchy taglines/WhatsApp/push copy, and **dual Arabic + Latin Darija subtitles** on short-form videos.

## 5. Hard Anti-Slop Prose Audit (Run Before Every Delivery)

Rewrite any draft that fails even one check:
- **Kill AI Openers & Crutches:** Delete *"In today's fast-paced landscape"*, *"Here's the thing:"*, *"Let's dive in"*, *"seamlessly"*, *"empower"*, *"game-changer"*, *"unlock"*, and all adverbs doing weak work.
- **Ban Formulaic Contrasts:** Zero *"It's not just X—it's Y"* or *"No X. No Y. Just Z."* staccato fragments.
- **Active Human Subjects:** Replace passive/abstract phrasing (*"Efficiency is achieved"* → *"Your clerk reconciles 120 invoices in 8 minutes"*).
- **Specificity Over Superlatives:** Replace *"fast onboarding"* with *"10-minute `.mae` file import"*; strip em-dashes and vary sentence length.

## Verification Checklist
- [ ] Draft names a concrete ICP, uses buyer-native vocabulary, and contains at least one verifiable number or mechanism detail.
- [ ] Passes all 4 Anti-Slop rules; zero invented quotes, stats, or unapproved pricing claims.
